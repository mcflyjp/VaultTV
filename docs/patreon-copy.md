# VaultTV Patreon Copy

Draft copy for the VaultTV Patreon page — tiers, about blurb, and pinned
welcome post. Core rule: **no tier ever gates a feature, format, or
capability the free version doesn't have.** Tiers are recognition +
access-to-me, not a paywall.

## Tiers

### 🗝️ Tier 1 — Vault Keeper — $3/mo
- Early access to new builds before public release
- Name in the in-app Supporters list
- Vote on what gets built next (roadmap polls)
- A warm, glowing sense of having made this thing possible

### 🛡️ Tier 2 — Vault Guardian — $8/mo
Everything in Vault Keeper, plus:
- Priority on feature requests — yours get looked at first
- Direct line to me (Discord/DM) for bugs, ideas, or "why doesn't X work"
- A supporter badge next to your name in the app
- First look at experimental/beta features (opt-in, your call)

*No tier ever unlocks a feature, format, or capability the free version
doesn't have. That's not up for negotiation — it's what makes this worth
supporting.*

## About page blurb

**VaultTV** is a personal streaming hub I built because I was tired of
juggling five different apps to watch what I own, what I've collected
through Stremio add-ons, and what's sitting on my hard drive. One app, four
places to run it — desktop, self-hosted media server, web, and (soon)
FireTV — all syncing your watch history and library across every device you
use.

It's built solo, nights and weekends, with zero VC money and zero interest
in turning it into another subscription service you resent paying for.
Everything works fully free, no asterisks. If you'd like to help keep it
moving — new features, bug fixes, actual FireTV support — that's what this
page is for.

What you *won't* find here: paywalls, feature-gating, or "upgrade to
unlock" nags. Just a tip jar with some nice extras attached.

## Pinned welcome post — "Welcome to the Vault"

Hey — thanks for being here, genuinely.

VaultTV started as a single Python script that scraped my Stremio add-ons
into something I could actually browse like a real app. It's grown into a
full app across desktop, a self-hosted server, and the web, with FireTV
support actively in the works. Every bit of that happened because I kept
chipping away at it in whatever spare time I had.

A few things to know as a new supporter:

- **Nothing is locked behind a tier.** Every feature in VaultTV is free for
  every user, always. Your support just funds the time I put into it and
  gets you a few nice extras (early builds, a say in what's next, a direct
  line to me).
- **Roadmap right now:** stabilizing FireTV, a real 1.0 launch on
  desktop/Android, and whatever you all vote up next.
- **Found a bug? Have an idea?** Don't wait for a poll — just tell me. This
  app exists because people actually use it and tell me what's broken.

Glad you're here. Let's build this thing out together.
